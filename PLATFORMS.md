## Launch Platforms

### Tier 1: High Reach, Free

#### Product Hunt
- **URL:** https://www.producthunt.com
- **What it is:** The dominant product discovery platform. Products compete on a daily upvote leaderboard.
- **Reach:** ~5.4M monthly visits. DR 91. 500K email subscribers. 70% male audience, largest group 25-34.
- **Cost:** Free
- **Criteria:** Account must be 1+ week old. Need live product URL, tagline, 3+ screenshots (no stock photos). No coming-soon pages.
- **Best for:** SaaS, dev tools, consumer apps, browser extensions — anything with visual appeal and broad tech interest.
- **Submission:** Log in → Submit → fill product details → launches at 12:01 AM PST. Best days: Wed > Tue > Thu.
- **Backlinks:** Nofollow
- **Tips:** Find a hunter with followers in your niche. Share PH link on social. Respond to every comment in first 24h.

#### Hacker News (Show HN)
- **URL:** https://news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html
- **What it is:** Y Combinator's tech link aggregator. "Show HN" is for sharing things you've built.
- **Reach:** ~10M+ monthly visits. DR 91. Front-page Show HN can yield ~11K visitors and 300+ signups in a day.
- **Cost:** Free
- **Criteria:** Must be something people can run/use. No sign-up-only pages, newsletters, or marketing material. No vote brigading. Username shouldn't be company name.
- **Best for:** Developer tools, open-source, technically interesting products. Audience is deeply technical.
- **Submission:** Submit URL with "Show HN:" prefix title. Add backstory comment. Best timing: weekday mornings US EST.
- **Backlinks:** Nofollow
- **Tips:** Be genuine, respond to every comment, don't be defensive about criticism. Technical details win here.

#### dev.to (DEV Community)
- **URL:** https://dev.to
- **What it is:** Largest online dev community (Forem-powered, owned by Major League Hacking). Content-based platform.
- **Reach:** 15M+ monthly unique visitors. 1M+ registered devs. DR 80.
- **Cost:** Free
- **Criteria:** Anyone can publish. Posts should be genuinely helpful, not pure marketing. Write tutorials/educational content featuring your product.
- **Best for:** Developer tools, APIs, open-source. Requires content creation, not just a listing.
- **Backlinks:** Dofollow
- **Tips:** Write educational content, tag appropriately. Community engagement drives visibility.

#### Peerlist Launchpad
- **URL:** https://peerlist.io/launchpad
- **What it is:** Professional network for tech people with weekly product launches. "Proof of work" portfolios + product discovery.
- **Reach:** ~400K monthly visits. DR 76. Rankings randomized for first 2 days for fair exposure.
- **Cost:** Free
- **Criteria:** Open to any project. Launches on Mondays. All submitted projects get featured.
- **Best for:** Developer tools, startups, side projects targeting devs and makers.
- **Backlinks:** Nofollow

### Tier 2: Established, Free/Cheap

#### BetaList
- **URL:** https://betalist.com
- **What it is:** Platform for discovering and getting early access to upcoming startups. Accepts pre-launch and recently launched.
- **Reach:** DR 75. ~11K monthly organic. 15-20% conversion rates. Typical: 200-500 visitors, 30-100 signups per launch.
- **Cost:** **Effectively paid.** FAQ mentions a free queue option, but the submission flow requires selecting a paid package. $129+ for expedited review.
- **Criteria:** Recently launched or unreleased. Each startup gets two featuring opportunities: pre-launch and launch.
- **Best for:** Pre-launch and early-stage startups seeking early adopters and beta testers.
- **Backlinks:** Dofollow

#### BetaPage
- **URL:** https://betapage.co
- **What it is:** Startup discovery at any stage. Helps get feedback, beta subscriptions, early adopters.
- **Reach:** 57-65K monthly visitors. 30-45K newsletter subscribers. 40K+ listed products. DR 69.
- **Cost:** Free to submit. ~$60 featured (homepage + newsletter).
- **Criteria:** Accepts any stage. Need 5+ upvotes to appear in "Recent" section.
- **Best for:** Startups at any stage seeking feedback and early adopters.
- **Backlinks:** Dofollow

#### LaunchingNext
- **URL:** https://www.launchingnext.com
- **What it is:** Human-curated startup directory running since 2013. New tech startups, side projects, business ideas.
- **Reach:** 45K+ listed startups. 25K+ social followers. 18K+ newsletter subscribers. DR 51.
- **Cost:** Free
- **Criteria:** Open to tech startups, side projects, business ideas. Human-curated.
- **Best for:** Early-stage startups seeking initial users, feedback, and SEO.
- **Backlinks:** Dofollow

#### KillerStartups
- **URL:** https://killerstartups.com
- **What it is:** Community for startup founders. Get listed or reviewed.
- **Reach:** 125K+ unique monthly views. 6M total annual views.
- **Cost:** Free
- **Criteria:** Must answer all submission questions completely. Incomplete submissions denied.
- **Best for:** Any early-stage startup.
- **Backlinks:** Mixed

#### SideProjectors
- **URL:** https://www.sideprojectors.com
- **What it is:** Marketplace/community to buy, sell, and showcase side projects.
- **Reach:** Developer/entrepreneur community. DR 69.
- **Cost:** Free. No listing fees, no commission.
- **Criteria:** Open to any side project/startup. Can list for sale, seek co-founders, or showcase.
- **Best for:** Side projects, developer tools, indie products.
- **Backlinks:** Dofollow

#### Startup Stash
- **URL:** https://startupstash.com
- **What it is:** World's largest curated directory of tools/resources for startups. Most upvoted product on PH.
- **Reach:** Hundreds of thousands monthly users. DR 66.
- **Cost:** Free. Paid options for faster review/premium placement.
- **Criteria:** Must be a tool or resource useful for startups.
- **Best for:** SaaS tools, productivity software, startup resources.
- **Backlinks:** Nofollow

### Tier 3: Indie/Emerging

#### Uneed
- **URL:** https://www.uneed.best
- **What it is:** Growing product discovery with daily launch competitions. Smaller, more engaged community than PH.
- **Reach:** 22K visitors/month. 250K page views. 15K newsletter subscribers (40% open rate). DR 74.
- **Cost:** Free (queue) / $29.99 skip queue / $15 relaunch / $147 newsletter sponsor.
- **Criteria:** Submit product details. Launches at 12:00 AM PST. 8-15 products compete daily.
- **Best for:** Indie makers, SaaS, dev tools.
- **Backlinks:** Dofollow

#### Firsto
- **URL:** https://firsto.co
- **What it is:** Launch platform for indie makers. Every product gets homepage visibility (no selection lottery).
- **Reach:** ~32K monthly visitors. DR 57-59.
- **Cost:** Free (queue ~180 days) / $19.90 premium (24hr launch) / $59.90 pro (30-day homepage) / $149 SEO review article.
- **Criteria:** Daily launches at 8:00 UTC.
- **Best for:** Indie makers wanting sustained visibility. Category-based search keeps products discoverable.
- **Backlinks:** Dofollow (paid tiers)

#### Microlaunch
- **URL:** https://microlaunch.net
- **What it is:** 30-day leaderboard cycles (vs PH's 24-hour). Sustained discoverability.
- **Reach:** 15-25K monthly visitors. ~14.5% traffic share for featured products. ~3% conversion.
- **Cost:** Starting at $49/month. No free tier.
- **Criteria:** Accepts ideas and fully developed products, scored separately.
- **Best for:** Early-stage products benefiting from sustained visibility.
- **Backlinks:** Dofollow

#### Smol Launch
- **URL:** https://smollaunch.com
- **What it is:** Weekly launch platform. Supportive, low-competition environment.
- **Reach:** Growing. Thousands of tech-savvy early adopters.
- **Cost:** Free. Premium spot available.
- **Criteria:** Open to indie makers, bootstrapped startups, seed-funded builders.
- **Best for:** First-time launchers wanting a full week of visibility.
- **Backlinks:** Dofollow

#### DevHunt
- **URL:** https://devhunt.org
- **What it is:** Product Hunt alternative specifically for developer tools. Uses GitHub PRs for listings.
- **Reach:** Growing. Each submission gets a Google-indexed product page.
- **Cost:** Free. Paid options for expedited processing.
- **Criteria:** Must be a developer tool.
- **Best for:** Dev tools, CLIs, SDKs, APIs.
- **Backlinks:** Dofollow

#### OpenHunts
- **URL:** https://openhunts.com
- **What it is:** Community-focused product launch and discovery for makers/developers.
- **Reach:** 500+ founders. 14.3% conversion rate reported.
- **Cost:** Free
- **Criteria:** Tech products, startups, dev tools. Curated.
- **Best for:** Makers and developers.
- **Backlinks:** —

#### Lobste.rs
- **URL:** https://lobste.rs
- **What it is:** Invite-only computing-focused community. High-quality, anti-spam ethos.
- **Reach:** 20K+ visitors/weekday (~400K+/month). Highly engaged technical audience.
- **Cost:** Free (invite-only — need existing member to vouch).
- **Criteria:** Self-promotion must be <25% of submissions. New users have restricted permissions.
- **Best for:** Open-source, dev tools, CompSci-oriented. Not for consumer/marketing-heavy products.
- **Backlinks:** Nofollow

#### StartuPage
- **URL:** https://startupa.ge
- **What it is:** All-in-one startup platform with verified revenue profiles, investor matching, co-founder search.
- **Reach:** Growing. Unique in revenue verification.
- **Cost:** Free tier. No credit card required.
- **Criteria:** Open to startups. Revenue verified through payment processor integration (Stripe, etc.).
- **Best for:** Early-stage founders wanting verified traction credibility.

### Tier 4: AI-Specific Launch

- **Best for:** Any AI tool. Higher intent traffic than Product Hunt.

- **Criteria:** Must be an AI tool.

### Tier 5: Newsletter & Marketplace

#### TLDR Newsletter
- **URL:** https://tldr.tech (ads: https://advertise.tldr.tech)
- **What it is:** Daily tech newsletter. 3 ad slots per issue. TLDR's team writes your ad copy.
- **Reach:** 1.25M+ subscribers. Primary: 600-1K clicks. Dev: 500-700 clicks. Audience: devs, PMs, marketers, execs.
- **Cost:** Primary: $15K/issue. Secondary: $10K. Third: $5K. Includes copywriting + reports.
- **Best for:** Products with budget for paid acquisition targeting tech professionals.

#### Ben's Bites
- **URL:** https://bensbites.com (community: https://news.bensbites.com)
- **What it is:** Twice-weekly AI-focused newsletter. Community voting board.
- **Reach:** 120K+ subscribers. AI builders and founders.
- **Cost:** Free community submission. Paid sponsorship available (contact for pricing).
- **Best for:** AI tools, AI-powered products.
- **Submission:** Submit to news.bensbites.com. Top-voted get newsletter inclusion.

#### AppSumo
- **URL:** https://sell.appsumo.com
- **What it is:** Largest software deals marketplace. Lifetime deals to massive audience.
- **Reach:** 1.25M+ followers. 5K+ affiliates. ~2M monthly visits. Top performers get up to $25K ad spend.
- **Cost:** Free to apply. Revenue share ~60-70%. No upfront cost.
- **Criteria:** Working product (not beta). Real problem solved. Stable business. SMB overlap. Top 1% accepted for white-glove.
- **Best for:** SaaS, productivity, marketing tools. Must be willing to offer lifetime deal.
- **Submission:** Apply at sell.appsumo.com. If accepted, ~60 day campaign with full marketing.

#### BetaTesting (formerly Erlibird)
- **URL:** https://betatesting.com
- **What it is:** Beta testing platform connecting products with 400K real-world testers.
- **Reach:** 400K registered testers.
- **Cost:** Credits $20-$39 each. Monthly: $170-$580. Annual: $2.7K-$60K. Enterprise: $20K+/year.
- **Best for:** Products needing structured testing, usability feedback, bug reports. Validation over visibility.

### Major Review Platforms (DR 88+)

#### G2
- **URL:** https://www.g2.com
- **What it is:** World's largest B2B software review marketplace. 5.5M+ buyers.
- **DR:** 91 | **Traffic:** ~789K/mo | **Link:** Dofollow
- **Cost:** Free basic listing. Paid for enhanced profiles, badges, lead generation.
- **Criteria:** Real software product. Reviews from verified users needed for badges/ranking. **B2B only — B2C products are explicitly not eligible.**
- **Best for:** B2B SaaS, enterprise software.
- **Audience:** B2B only. Consumer/B2C products will be rejected on submission.
- **Notes:** Acquired Capterra/GetApp/Software Advice in Feb 2026. First 10 reviews are hardest but compound.

#### Capterra (G2-owned)

- **What it is:** Software comparison platform (formerly Gartner Digital Markets, now G2).

- **Cost:** Free listing. Paid PPC for featured placement.
- **Best for:** Mid-market B2B buyers, SaaS.
- **Audience:** B2B only (same restriction as G2). B2C products not eligible.

#### GetApp (G2-owned)

#### Software Advice (G2-owned)
